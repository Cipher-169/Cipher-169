### 🌊 About me

> *"I believe that curiosity is the best engine for growth."*

I'm a student from Feira de Santana, Bahia, currently pursuing Information Systems at IFBA. My journey goes beyond the classroom — I'm someone who loves to explore different areas at the same time, always looking for new challenges.

I started programming with Python and have been building web projects that connect to real APIs, from personal trackers to a digital catalog for a store.

When I'm not coding, you'll probably find me reading, listening to music, or diving into anything related to Japan and anime. I'm also on a personal adventure of learning English, Japanese, and piano — because why stop at one thing when there's so much to discover?

Mathematics has always fascinated me, and I believe it connects naturally with programming. My goal is to become a great developer and build a career doing what I truly love.
